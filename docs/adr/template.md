# NNNN — Short, decision-shaped title

Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN

## Context

What situation forced a decision? What were the actual constraints (not
every option in the abstract, just the ones on the table)? A sentence or
two — enough that someone unfamiliar with the moment understands why
this wasn't obvious.

## Decision

The choice, stated plainly, in one or two sentences. Not a design doc —
the decision itself.

## Consequences

What you get, and what you're giving up to get it. Be honest about the
costs — a decision record that only lists upsides isn't trustworthy, and
won't help the next person evaluate whether circumstances have changed
enough to revisit it.
